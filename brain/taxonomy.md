# Business-function taxonomy

Sixteen functions in three tiers. Every signal and opportunity card carries exactly one node, so
results can be compared by function. Supplied by Archie (decision D7, 2026-11-16).

## Tier 1 — revenue-critical
- sales
- marketing
- customer service and success
- operations and delivery
- supply chain and logistics
- product and R&D

## Tier 2 — control-critical
- finance
- legal, risk and compliance
- IT and security
- HR

## Tier 3 — enabling
- procurement
- data and analytics
- strategy and M&A
- communications, IR and ESG
- facilities and property
- transformation office
